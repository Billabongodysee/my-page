From Woodcraft to Creations

MLP Creations started as MLP Woodcraft, focused on end grain cutting boards and outdoor furniture. As the shop grew, so did what came out of it — a CNC router opened the door to signs, bowls, and trays, and a fiber laser added custom engraving on PMAGs and handgun slides. The new name reflects where the shop actually is now: still rooted in woodworking, but not limited to it.

Every piece is still made in-house, in small batches, with an emphasis on getting the details right — tight glue-ups, finishes that hold up to real use, and engraving work that's precise enough to trust with a customer's own firearm or gear.
