New Service: Fiber Laser Engraving Is Here

We picked up a fiber laser this year, and it's opened up a couple of new services we're excited about: custom engraving on PMAGs and handgun slides.

PMAG engraving is the simpler of the two — send us your design idea (or pick from a few stock styles), and we'll lay it out and engrave it on a Magpul PMAG, either one you send in or one we supply. Turnaround is usually just a few days.

Handgun slide engraving is a bigger undertaking and is handled by appointment only. Because it involves a firearm, the process starts with a design consultation, followed by shipping the firearm to us through a licensed FFL — we're happy to walk you through exactly how that works when you reach out. As always, it's on the customer to make sure the order complies with all applicable federal, state, and local firearms laws.

Both services are now listed in the shop under Laser Engraving. If you've got a design in mind, get in touch and we'll get you a quote.
