Custom Sign Orders: How It Works

Custom signs are one of our most popular CNC projects, and they're easier to order than people expect. Here's the general process:

1. Tell us the idea. Wording, rough size, and where it'll hang (indoors vs. outdoors changes what wood and finish we recommend). A reference photo or sketch helps but isn't required.

2. We send a proof. Before anything gets cut, you'll see a digital layout of the sign so you can check wording, spacing, and font before it's committed to wood.

3. We carve and finish. Once the proof is approved, the design gets cut on the CNC, sanded, and finished — painted lettering, routed and stained, or left natural, depending on the look you want.

4. Pickup or shipping. Small and mid-sized signs ship well; larger pieces may be local pickup only depending on size.

Most custom signs take one to two weeks from approved proof to finished piece, longer during busy seasons like the run-up to the holidays. Reach out with your idea and we'll get you a quote and timeline.
