Building an Adirondack Chair, Start to Finish

Adirondack chairs look simple, but there's more to a good one than the classic silhouette. Ours start with weather-resistant lumber — cedar or a similarly rot-resistant species — since these chairs are meant to live outside year-round, not just get pulled out in the summer.

Each piece is cut and shaped on the CNC first for consistency, then hand-sanded and assembled with stainless or exterior-rated hardware so nothing rusts and streaks the wood down the road. The curved back slats and wide armrests are what make the chair comfortable to actually sit in for a while, not just look good on a porch.

Finish-wise, we use an exterior-grade stain and sealer built for sun and moisture exposure. Chairs are built individually or as matched pairs, so a set you order together will look like it was always meant to be together — same wood, same finish batch.

From cut list to a finished chair usually takes about a week, longer for multi-chair orders. If you want a specific stain color to match an existing deck or fence, let us know when you order.
